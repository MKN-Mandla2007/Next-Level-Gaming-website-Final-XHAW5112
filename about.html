Our Story

The Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is an initiative based in Johannesburg that was founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo. Jason is a passionate gamer who wanted to give everyone the chance to experience the same wonderfully creative, unique world of video games.

We aim to provide people of all demographics the potential to enjoy unique worlds, art, and out-of-this-world mechanical experiences at a fraction of its usual price. If more people can feel transported to a brand new world when they visit us, then we feel we have achieved our goal.

Who We Serve

Casual Players

The kind of people who just want to blow off some steam or have fun with their friends. Pure entertainment.

Competitive Gamers

Train and compete in our tournament-ready setups. Join leagues, scrimmages, and ranked events.

Schools

Educational gaming programs, after-school clubs, and supervised group sessions for young players.

Corporate Entities

Team building, product launches, and private events in our fully equipped gaming venue.
