This is the Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena Website.

What is this?

This is a website I built for a business called Next Level Gaming & Esports
Arena. It’s a place in Johannesburg where people can go to use high-end gaming
PCs, play VR, or host birthday parties and corporate events.

The goal of the website is to let customers see what the arena offers, learn
about the business, and get a price quote for the experiences they want to book.

What can you do on the site?

I made several pages to make the site easy to use:

  - Home Page: A welcome screen that shows a glimpse of the best packages.
  - About Us: A page that tells the story of the founder, Jason Naidoo, and
    explains who the arena is for (like casual gamers or schools).
  - Packages/Products: A list of all the "deals" available. I split these into
    two groups: the big Gaming Packages (R1,500) and the smaller Individual
    Experiences (R750).
  - Contact Us: A page with a map and a form so people can reach out to the
    business.
  - Fee Calculator: This is the most important part of the site.

How the Calculator Works (The Simple Version)

I wanted to make it easy for customers to see how much they would pay before
they book. Here is how the logic works:

1.  User Info: The user puts in their name, email, and phone number. (If they
    leave these blank, the site tells them they made a mistake).
2.  Picking Items: The user checks boxes for the packages they want.
3.  The Discount: To encourage people to book more, I added a "Multi-Booking"
    discount:
      - 1 item = No discount.
      - 2 items = 5% off.
      - 3 items = 10% off.
      - 4 or more items = 15% off.
4.  The Total: The site adds up the prices, takes away the discount, and then
    adds 15% VAT (tax) to give the final Grand Total.

How to run the website

You don't need to install anything fancy to see the site:

1.  Download the project folder.
2.  Find the file called index.html.
3.  Double-click it to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, etc.).
